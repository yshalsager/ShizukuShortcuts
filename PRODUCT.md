# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Android power users and automation users who already use Shizuku and want fast launcher, widget, and in-app access to privileged actions. The interface serves English and Arabic users, including RTL layouts, and should remain usable during brief one-handed interactions.

## Product Purpose

Shizuku Shortcuts makes a small set of system and custom shell actions immediate without requiring users to reopen a terminal or automation setup. Success means actions are quick, reliable, understandable, and unobtrusive whether launched from the app, launcher, widget, or another app.

## Brand Personality

Focused, technical, calm. The app should feel capable and trustworthy without becoming visually loud or resembling a dense developer console.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy decoration, novelty motion, cramped terminal-style interfaces, tiny controls, excessive status badges, and generic dashboard clutter. Technical power should not require visual complexity.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary action immediate and the interface out of the way.
- Show one clear system state and the next useful action.
- Preserve user control around privileged or destructive behavior.
- Prefer familiar Android interaction patterns over custom substitutes.
- Treat English, Arabic RTL, and large text as equal layouts rather than adaptations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, provide at least 48 dp interactive targets, support large text without clipping, preserve correct English and Arabic RTL behavior, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
